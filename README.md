# MCA Capitals

**MCA Capitals** is a Forge addon for **Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn** that adds a working monarchy and capital system to MCA villages.

Villages can grow into capitals, appoint sovereigns, develop royal courts, recognize heirs, elevate nobles, assign court offices, record major events, and create a deeper political story around MCA families and villages.

This mod is designed for players who want their MCA worlds to feel more dynastic, political, and alive.

---

## Features

### Capitals

Villages can become **Capitals** once they reach the required population. When a village qualifies, a **Royal Charter** is issued, allowing the player to decide the future of the settlement.

A capital can:

- Appoint a villager sovereign.
- Allow the player to claim the throne.
- Reject monarchy.
- Develop a royal court over time.
- Record important events in a Capital Chronicle.
- Generate capital-specific villager dialogue and court news.

---

### Monarchy

Each capital can have a ruling sovereign.

Sovereigns may be:

- **King**
- **Queen**
- **High King**
- **High Queen**

A player may become sovereign by claiming the throne through the Royal Charter, petitioning for the throne, or seizing the throne.

Villagers may become sovereign through appointment, succession, abdication, or other capital power transfers.

---

### Royal Families and Succession

MCA Capitals builds on MCA Reborn’s family systems to support royal succession and dynastic titles.

The mod includes:

- Crown Princes and Crown Princesses
- Heirs Apparent
- Princes and Princesses
- Consorts
- Dowagers
- Royal children
- Disinheritance
- Legitimization
- Abdication
- Succession after death or transfer of power

Succession generally prioritizes a valid named heir first, then dynastic royal children, then fallback candidates if no direct successor exists.

---

### Court Roles

Capitals can unlock and assign special court offices as the population grows.

Court roles include:

- **Hand of the King / Hand of the Queen**
- **Lord Commander**
- **Grand Maester**
- **Court Herald**
- **Royal Guards**
- **Dukes and Duchesses**
- **Lords and Ladies**
- **Knights and Dames**

These roles affect title display, dialogue, court events, capital identity, and in some cases gameplay behaviour.

---

### Petitions

Players can petition the reigning sovereign for status or power.

Available petitions include:

- Petition for Lord/Lady
- Petition for Duke/Duchess
- Petition for Lord Commander
- Petition for the Throne

Petitions require enough capital standing with the village and, for some titles, a minimum capital population.

---

### Seizing the Throne

Players who meet the requirements can attempt to seize the throne.

Seizing the throne is a forceful path to power and requires:

- Strong capital standing
- The **Cover Me in Diamonds** advancement
- Support from the Lord Commander, or being the Lord Commander yourself
- Access to the current sovereign

A successful seizure transfers the throne and records the event in the Capital Chronicle.

---

### Mourning

When important capital figures die, the capital may enter mourning.

During mourning:

- Villagers may wear mourning clothing.
- Royal, noble, and commoner mourning tiers are handled separately.
- Mourning lasts for a limited in-game period.
- Villagers return to their previous clothing after mourning ends when possible.

---

### Capital Chronicle

The **Capital Chronicle** records major events in a capital’s history.

It may record events such as:

- Capital founding
- Sovereign appointment
- Throne seizure
- Abdication
- Succession
- Royal marriage
- Royal birth
- Court appointments
- Noble elevation
- Disinheritance
- Legitimization
- Mourning
- Betrothals
- Marriages

---

### Royal Decrees

The mod adds several royal documents and seals.

Documents include:

- **Royal Charter**
- **Betrothal Decree**
- **Royal Disinheritance**
- **Legitimization Decree**
- **Declaration of Abdication**
- **Capital Chronicle**

Royal Seals are used as crafting components for several documents.

---

### Capital Dialogue

MCA villagers can speak capital-aware dialogue based on their rank, role, and current capital events.

Dialogue categories include:

- General capital dialogue
- Sovereign dialogue
- Royal child dialogue
- Noble dialogue
- Commoner dialogue
- Court role dialogue
- Event news
- Mourning news

Babies and toddlers are blocked from using adult capital dialogue.

---

## Requirements

MCA Capitals requires:

- **Minecraft 1.20.1**
- **Forge 47.3.1+**
- **Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn 7.6.16+1.20.1**
- **Architectury API 9.2.14+**

This release is intended for **Forge**.

Other loaders are not supported for this release.

---

## Recommended Setup

Install the following mods together:

1. Minecraft Forge for 1.20.1
2. Architectury API
3. Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn
4. MCA Capitals

Back up your worlds before adding MCA Capitals to an existing MCA Reborn world.

---

## Compatibility

MCA Capitals depends heavily on MCA Reborn systems, including:

- MCA villagers
- MCA village data
- MCA family trees
- MCA relationships
- MCA marriage data
- MCA professions
- MCA guard and archer roles
- MCA clothing systems

Because of this, MCA Capitals should be used with the supported MCA Reborn version listed above.
